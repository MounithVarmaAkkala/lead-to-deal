1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & METADATA
================================================================================

Elevator Pitch
--------------
An autonomous, real-time lead-to-deal intelligence system that ingests lead and
engagement events, continuously updates lead scores and pipeline stages,
auto-assigns and prioritizes leads via round-robin + score/recency, and provides
fine-grained human-readable explanations for every score change and recommended
action. Built for transparency, speed (< 500 ms), and multi-tenant SaaS readiness.

2. PROBLEM DEFINITION & STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT
================================================================================

User Pain Points
----------------
- Sales teams waste hours manually scoring and prioritizing leads.
- Lack of transparency in existing scoring tools erodes trust
  (“why did this lead jump 30 points?”).
- Pipeline stages lag behind real engagement.
- High-priority leads get stuck when reps are at capacity.

Market Context & Why Now
------------------------
Modern revenue teams demand real-time, explainable intelligence. Existing CRMs
either lack autonomous scoring or hide the logic. Competitors are moving toward
AI-assisted pipelines.

Why Rule-Based Scoring First (v1)
---------------------------------
We deliberately choose pure rule-based scoring with explicit weighted formulas
for v1 because:

- Trust & Adoption: Sales teams will not use a black-box model. Fine-grained
  explanations (“+12 points because of 3 email opens in last 48h”) are
  non-negotiable for early buy-in.
- Speed to Value: Rule engine can be shipped, tuned, and audited in weeks,
  not months.
- Debuggability & Compliance: Every score change is fully reproducible and
  auditable — critical for regulated or enterprise sales orgs.
- Foundation for ML: Clean, labeled event + explanation data collected in v1
  becomes the perfect training set for hybrid/ML models later.

Out of Scope for v1.0
---------------------
- Machine-learning / predictive models
- Advanced territory or skill-based assignment
- Deal-value estimation
- Full SSO / advanced RBAC
- Mobile native apps
- Custom pipeline stage configuration UI
- Email/SMS outreach automation

3. USER PERSONAS & USER SCENARIOS
================================================================================

Primary Persona – Sales Representative (Alex)
---------------------------------------------
Goal: Focus only on the highest-priority, hottest leads.
Motivation: Close more deals with less manual work.
Journey: Logs in → sees prioritized “Hot Queue” + personal queue → clicks lead
→ views live score history + explanations → takes action → stage auto-updates.

Secondary Persona – Sales Manager / Admin (Jordan)
--------------------------------------------------
Goal: Oversee pipeline health, assignment fairness, and scoring transparency.
Motivation: Maximize team conversion rate and reduce lead leakage.
Journey: Dashboard overview → monitor assignment load → review score change
explanations → adjust capacity rules → audit closed-won/lost reasons.

Tertiary Persona – Sales Ops Analyst (Taylor)
---------------------------------------------
Goal: Continuously audit scoring fairness, assignment balance, and weight
effectiveness; tune the system without engineering tickets.
Motivation: Maximize ROI of the lead engine and protect data quality.
Journey: Reviews explanation audit logs and assignment fairness reports →
adjusts scoring weights via Admin UI → monitors impact on conversion and
hot-queue metrics → reports insights to leadership.

End-to-End Journey Map (Sales Rep)
----------------------------------
1. Lead created or engagement event arrives.
2. System scores, stages, assigns, and pushes update via WebSocket.
3. Rep sees updated priority queue in < 500 ms.
4. Opens lead → full explanation audit trail visible.
5. Takes action (call, email, meeting).
6. New event triggers next score/stage update.

4. USER STORIES & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (Gherkin)
================================================================================

US-01 – Real-time Score Update
As a Sales Rep, I want lead scores to update instantly when an engagement event
arrives, so that I always work with the freshest priority.

Given a lead exists with score 45
When an “email open” event is ingested
Then the score increases by the configured weight
And a fine-grained explanation is recorded
    (“+X points because of email open in last 48h”)
And the updated score + explanation is pushed via WebSocket to all relevant
    clients within 500 ms

US-02 – Automatic Forward Stage Transition
As a Sales Rep, I want pipeline stages to advance automatically when score
thresholds or key events are met, so that the pipeline stays current.

Given a lead is in stage “Contacted” and score crosses the Qualified threshold
When the scoring engine evaluates the lead
Then the stage moves to “Qualified”
And an explanation is logged
And the change is broadcast in real time

US-03 – Manual Confirmation for Backward or Closed Stages
As a Sales Rep, I want to confirm any backward movement or Closed-Won/Lost
transition, so that critical decisions remain human-controlled.

Given a lead is in “Qualified”
When a user attempts to move it back to “Contacted” or to Closed-Won/Lost
Then a confirmation dialog is required
And the transition only occurs after confirmation

US-04 – Round-Robin Assignment + Hot Queue
As a Sales Manager, I want new leads assigned round-robin and high-priority
leads placed in a hot queue when capacity is full, so that no hot lead is dropped.

Given multiple available Sales Reps
When a new lead is scored
Then it is assigned to the next rep in round-robin order
And if all reps are at capacity, the lead enters the “Hot Queue”
And when any rep frees capacity, the highest-priority hot-queue lead is reassigned

US-05 – Fine-grained Explanation Audit
As a Sales Rep or Admin, I want every score change to have a clear,
human-readable reason, so that I trust and can explain the system.

Given any score-changing event
When the scoring engine runs
Then an immutable audit record is created containing:
  - previous score
  - new score
  - delta
  - exact feature(s) and weights applied
  - timestamp
  - triggering event ID

US-06 – Admin Weight Tuning
As an Admin / Sales Ops Analyst, I want to adjust scoring weights via a simple
UI, so that I can tune the system without requiring engineering changes.

Given I am authenticated as Admin
When I open the Scoring Weights page
Then I can view current weights and time windows for every feature
And I can update any weight or window
And the change takes effect on the next event evaluation
And an audit entry is written recording who changed what and when

5. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (FRONTEND & BACKEND)
================================================================================

P0 – Must Have
--------------
- Ingest endpoint for all core events:
  lead created, email open/click, form submission, page visit, meeting booked,
  call logged, deal stage change.
- Idempotent event ingestion: Every event carries a unique event_id.
  Duplicate event_ids within a tenant are ignored (no double-scoring).
- Pure rule-based scoring engine with configurable weights (Admin only).
- Fine-grained explanation generation and persistent immutable audit log.
- Automatic forward stage transitions based on score thresholds + key events.
- Manual confirmation required for backward and Closed-Won/Lost transitions.
- Round-robin assignment among available reps.
- Capacity model: Maximum concurrent open leads per Sales Rep
  (configurable per user). When a rep reaches this limit they are marked
  unavailable for new assignments.
- Hot Queue when capacity is reached; automatic reassignment when capacity frees.
- Real-time WebSocket push of score, stage, assignment, and explanation
  updates (< 500 ms).
- Multi-tenant data isolation.
- JWT authentication + roles (Admin, Sales Rep).
- React dashboard: prioritized queue, lead detail with explanation timeline,
  pipeline view, hot queue.

P1 – Should Have
----------------
- Webhook receivers for HubSpot, Salesforce, Segment (basic signature validation).
- Admin UI to view/adjust scoring weights (implements US-06).
- Capacity settings per rep.
- Basic filtering and search on leads.

P2 – Nice to Have
-----------------
- Export of explanation audit logs.
- Simple notification preferences.

UI/UX Behaviors
---------------
- Score changes animate with color flash + delta badge.
- Explanation timeline is reverse-chronological and expandable.
- Hot Queue is visually distinct (red/amber).
- Empty states for new tenants and empty queues.
- Optimistic UI with rollback on WebSocket failure.

6. FULL-STACK TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & DATA MODEL
================================================================================

High-level Architecture
-----------------------
[Webhook / Demo Simulator]
          ↓
   FastAPI Ingest Layer  ←→  Redis (event buffer + rate limiting + pub/sub)
          ↓
   Scoring + Assignment Engine
          ↓
   PostgreSQL (multi-tenant, immutable audit tables)
          ↓
   WebSocket Broadcaster  ←→  React Clients

- Redis is in the core path for short-term event buffering under load spikes,
  rate limiting, and optional pub/sub for horizontal WebSocket scaling.
- Kafka is not required for v1 but the architecture leaves a clean extension point.

Confirmed Tech Stack
--------------------
Layer              | Technology
-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------
Backend            | Python 3.12 + FastAPI
Database           | PostgreSQL (primary). SQLite only for pure local dev.
ORM / Migrations   | SQLAlchemy 2.0 + Alembic
Real-time          | WebSockets (FastAPI native)
Auth               | JWT + simple RBAC (Admin, Sales Rep)
Event Ingest       | FastAPI endpoints + background workers
Frontend           | React 18 + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
Caching / Queue    | Redis
Deployment         | Docker Compose (local + cloud-ready)
Observability      | Structured logging + basic Prometheus metrics

Core Tables (PostgreSQL, multi-tenant)
--------------------------------------
- tenants (id, name, created_at)
- users (id, tenant_id, email, role, max_concurrent_leads, is_available, ...)
- leads (id, tenant_id, company_name, company_size, current_score, stage,
         assigned_to, priority_score, ...)
- events (id, tenant_id, lead_id, event_type, payload JSONB, received_at)
  → unique constraint on (tenant_id, event_id) for idempotency
- score_explanations (id, tenant_id, lead_id, event_id, previous_score,
                      new_score, delta, reason_text, features JSONB, created_at)
  → APPEND-ONLY / IMMUTABLE (no UPDATE or DELETE allowed; enforced by DB
     permissions + application logic)
- pipeline_transitions (id, lead_id, from_stage, to_stage, triggered_by,
                        confirmed_by, created_at)
- assignment_log (id, lead_id, from_user, to_user, reason, created_at)
- scoring_weights (tenant_id, feature_name, weight, window_hours, ...)
- weight_change_audit (id, tenant_id, changed_by, feature_name, old_weight,
                       new_weight, changed_at)

API Contract (selected)
-----------------------
- POST   /api/v1/events                          – idempotent ingest
- GET    /api/v1/leads                           – list with filters + priority sort
- GET    /api/v1/leads/{id}                      – detail + explanation timeline
- POST   /api/v1/leads/{id}/transition           – manual stage change (with confirmation)
- PUT    /api/v1/admin/scoring-weights           – Admin only
- WS     /ws/tenant/{tenant_id}                  – real-time channel

Security
--------
- JWT with short expiry + refresh
- Tenant isolation enforced at query level
- Webhook signature verification
- Rate limiting on ingest
- Input validation with Pydantic

7. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (NFRs)
================================================================================

- Latency: p95 event → WebSocket push < 500 ms.
- Throughput: Support 100 events/sec per tenant initially; horizontally scalable.
- Scalability: Horizontal scaling via Docker Compose (local) → Kubernetes
  (production). Stateless FastAPI workers + Redis + PostgreSQL read replicas.
- Availability: 99.5% for MVP.
- Multi-tenancy: Strict data isolation; no cross-tenant leakage.
- Privacy: GDPR/CCPA ready (data deletion endpoint, audit logs).
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance on the dashboard.
  Success criteria:
  - Full keyboard navigation
  - Screen-reader tested (VoiceOver / NVDA)
  - Color-contrast ≥ 4.5:1
  - Visible focus indicators
- Security: OWASP Top 10 mitigated; secrets in environment / vault.

================================================================================
8. EDGE CASES, ERROR HANDLING & FALLBACKS
================================================================================

- Duplicate events → ignored via (tenant_id, event_id) uniqueness.
- Invalid webhook signature → 401 + log.
- Scoring engine failure → event queued in Redis for retry; lead left in
  previous state.
- WebSocket disconnect → client auto-reconnects with last-known sequence.
- All reps at capacity → lead enters Hot Queue; notification to managers.
- Empty tenant → clear empty states + onboarding CTA.
- Concurrent stage transitions → optimistic locking / version column.
- Offline client → queue actions locally and sync when reconnected (best-effort).

10. OPEN QUESTIONS & RISKS
================================================================================

Open Questions
--------------
1. Exact numeric weights and stage thresholds (to be finalized with Sales Ops).
2. Do we need i18n for explanation text in v1?
   (Currently English-only; impacts explanation generation.)
3. Retention policy for audit logs and events — 90 days vs. 1 year vs. indefinite?
   (Directly affects database sizing and cost.)
4. Webhook payload mapping samples for HubSpot / Salesforce / Segment.
5. Whether capacity should also support a secondary daily assignment limit
   in addition to concurrent open leads.

Risks
-----
- Overly aggressive auto-staging may frustrate users → mitigated by clear
  explanations + easy undo.
- Round-robin can feel unfair under uneven load → Hot Queue + capacity
  visibility mitigate.
- Real-time performance under high multi-tenant load → Redis + horizontal
  scaling planned.
- Audit log growth → retention policy must be decided before GA.
